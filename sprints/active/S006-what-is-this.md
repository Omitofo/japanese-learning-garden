# S006 — What Is This?

## Seed

ask-what-something-is (L00 / early L03)

## Circumstance

You see an object (or something pointed out) and you do not know what it is called or what it is. You want a simple way to ask.

## Communicative objective

Be able to ask what something is, especially while pointing.

## Core phrase

これは何ですか。 (kore wa nan desu ka)

## Meaning

What is this? / What’s this?

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- これ (kore) — this (already known)
- は (wa) — topic particle
- 何 (nan / nani) — what
- です (desu) — polite copula (“is”)
- か (ka) — question particle

### Structure

これ + は + 何 + です + か。

This is one of the most useful question patterns in Japanese.

### Grammar / pattern (only what is needed)

- 何 (nan) is the form commonly used before です
- か (ka) turns the sentence into a question
- No rising English-style intonation is required; the か already marks the question

### Pronunciation

- これ (ko-re)
- は (wa)
- 何 (nan)
- です (de-su)
- か (ka)

Smooth unit: **kore wa nan desu ka**

### Context

- Extremely common when pointing at something unknown.
- Safe and polite in shops, restaurants, museums, everyday situations.
- Reuses これ from S001, so it connects directly with what you already know.

## Variations

Keep them few:

1. これは何ですか。 (kore wa nan desu ka) — this (near me)
2. それは何ですか。 (sore wa nan desu ka) — that (near you)
3. あれは何ですか。 (are wa nan desu ka) — that (over there)

You can later replace the demonstrative with a noun, but for now the three demonstratives are enough.

## Practice

1. **Observe**  
   Read: これは何ですか。 (kore wa nan desu ka)

2. **Break it**  
   これ … は … 何 … です … か

3. **Pronounce**  
   Say the full phrase several times until smooth.

4. **Hide**  
   Produce it from the meaning “What is this?”

5. **Transform**  
   Change the demonstrative:  
   - this → これは何ですか。  
   - that (near you) → それは何ですか。  
   - that (over there) → あれは何ですか。

6. **Situation**  
   You see something in a shop or on a menu and point to it.  
   Ask what it is.

7. **Link with previous seeds**  
   If you don’t understand the answer:  
   すみません、わかりません。 / ゆっくり話してください。 / もう一度お願いします。

## Proof of capability

Can you:

- Recognize the phrase?
- Say what it means?
- Produce これは何ですか。 without looking?
- Change it to それ / あれ?
- Use it while pointing at something?
- Fall back to your repair toolkit if you don’t understand the answer?

## Before

I had no simple way to ask what something was.

## After

I can point at something and ask これは何ですか。 (kore wa nan desu ka). I can also switch the demonstrative according to distance.

## Status

new → growing (after practice)

## Observations

- Strongly reuses これ / それ / あれ from S001.
- Opens the door to learning real vocabulary from the environment.
