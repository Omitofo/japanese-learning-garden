# S013 — Where Is the Station?

## Seed

ask-where-something-is (extension with concrete noun)

## Circumstance

You need to find the train station or subway station. You want a simple, polite way to ask where it is.

## Communicative objective

Be able to ask where the station is using a concrete noun.

## Core phrase

駅はどこですか。 (eki wa doko desu ka)

## Meaning

Where is the station?

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- 駅 (eki) — station (train / subway)
- は (wa) — topic particle
- どこ (doko) — where
- です (desu) — polite copula
- か (ka) — question particle

### Structure

駅 + は + どこ + です + か。

Exactly the same frame as トイレはどこですか. Only the concrete noun changes.

### Pronunciation

- 駅 (e-ki)

Smooth unit: **eki wa doko desu ka**

### Context

Extremely useful when arriving in a new area, getting off a bus, or looking for the nearest station. Polite and clear. One of the highest-value location questions for travel in Japan.

## Variations

Keep them few:

1. 駅はどこですか。 (eki wa doko desu ka) — standard version (recommended)
2. すみません、駅はどこですか。 (sumimasen, eki wa doko desu ka) — with polite attention-getter
3. 一番近い駅はどこですか。 (ichiban chikai eki wa doko desu ka) — “Where is the nearest station?” (optional extra, only if it feels natural)

## Practice

1. **Observe**  
   Read: 駅はどこですか。 (eki wa doko desu ka)

2. **Break it**  
   駅 … は … どこ … です … か

3. **Pronounce**  
   Say the full phrase several times until smooth.

4. **Hide**  
   Produce it from the meaning “Where is the station?”

5. **Compare with previous pattern**  
   - トイレはどこですか。 (where is the toilet?)  
   - 駅はどこですか。 (where is the station?)

6. **Situation**  
   You are in a new area and need the station. Ask where it is. Then thank the person.

7. **Full short chain**  
   すみません、駅はどこですか。 → (get answer) → ありがとうございます。

## Proof of capability

Can you:

- Recognize the phrase?
- Say what it means?
- Produce 駅はどこですか。 without looking?
- Add すみません in front when needed?
- Close with ありがとうございます after receiving the answer?

## Before

I could ask where the toilet is and where “this/that” is, but I had no high-value place name ready for travel.

## After

I can ask where the station is with 駅はどこですか。 (eki wa doko desu ka) and combine it with attention-getting and thanks.
