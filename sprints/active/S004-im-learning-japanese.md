# S004 — I’m Learning Japanese

## Seed

basic-repair (extension) / survival self-introduction

## Circumstance

You are in a real interaction and want to quickly explain that you are not a fluent speaker. You want the other person to understand that you are learning, so they can adjust (speak slower, use gestures, be patient).

## Communicative objective

Be able to politely say that you are learning Japanese / do not speak it well yet.

## Core phrase

すみません、日本語を勉強しています。 (sumimasen, nihongo o benkyou shiteimasu)

## Meaning

Sorry, I’m studying Japanese. / Excuse me, I’m learning Japanese.

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- すみません (sumimasen) — excuse me / sorry (already known)
- 日本語 (nihongo) — Japanese language
- を (o) — object particle
- 勉強しています (benkyou shiteimasu) — am studying / am learning (polite continuous form)

### Structure

すみません、 + 日本語を勉強しています。

### Grammar / pattern (only what is needed)

- 勉強する (benkyou suru) = to study
- 勉強しています (benkyou shiteimasu) = am studying (polite present continuous)

You only need the ready-made form for now.

### Pronunciation

- 日本語 (ni-hon-go)
- 勉強しています (ben-kyou shi-te i-ma-su)

Smooth unit: **sumimasen, nihongo o benkyou shiteimasu**

### Context

- Very useful at the beginning of an interaction or right after you get lost.
- Softens the situation and often makes the other person more helpful.
- Pairs well with わかりません and もう一度お願いします.

## Variations

Keep them few and useful:

1. すみません、日本語を勉強しています。 (sumimasen, nihongo o benkyou shiteimasu) — full recommended version
2. 日本語を勉強しています。 (nihongo o benkyou shiteimasu) — without the apology
3. 日本語がわかりません。 (nihongo ga wakarimasen) — I don’t understand Japanese (reuses わかりません)

## Practice

1. **Observe**  
   Read the core phrase and understand it.

2. **Break it**  
   すみません … 日本語 … を … 勉強しています

3. **Pronounce**  
   Say the full phrase several times until it feels smooth.

4. **Hide**  
   Produce it from the meaning “Sorry, I’m learning Japanese.”

5. **Chain with previous seeds**  
   Practice sequences such as:  
   - これください。 → (long reply) → すみません、日本語を勉強しています。  
   - すみません、わかりません。 → 日本語を勉強しています。 → もう一度お願いします。

6. **Situation**  
   Imagine you just entered a shop or someone started speaking fast.  
   Explain that you are learning.

## Proof of capability

Can you:

- Recognize the phrase?
- Say what it means?
- Produce it without looking?
- Use the shorter version?
- Insert it naturally into a survival chain with the previous phrases?

## Before

I had no simple way to explain that I am still learning Japanese.

## After

I can politely say that I am learning Japanese with すみません、日本語を勉強しています。 (sumimasen, nihongo o benkyou shiteimasu) and combine it with my existing repair tools.
