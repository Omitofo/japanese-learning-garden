# S010 — Ask for Water

## Seed

basic-request (extension with concrete noun)

## Circumstance

You are in a café, restaurant, convenience store, or any place where you want a drink of water. You want to ask for it clearly and politely without only pointing.

## Communicative objective

Be able to ask for water using a concrete noun + ください instead of only a demonstrative.

## Core phrase

水をください。 (mizu o kudasai)

## Meaning

Water, please. / Can I have some water?

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- 水 (mizu) — water
- を (o) — object particle (marks what you are asking for)
- ください (kudasai) — please (give me)

### Structure

水 + を + ください。

This is the same request frame as これください, only the demonstrative is replaced by a concrete noun and the object particle を is added for clarity.

### Pronunciation

- 水 (mi-zu)
- を (o)
- ください (ku-da-sa-i)

Smooth unit: **mizu o kudasai**

### Context

Extremely useful in restaurants, cafés, convenience stores, hotels, etc. Polite and clear. One of the highest-value survival requests a beginner can make.

## Variations

Keep them few:

1. 水をください。 (mizu o kudasai) — standard clear version (recommended)
2. お水をください。 (o-mizu o kudasai) — slightly more polite (the お prefix is very common with water)
3. すみません、水をください。 (sumimasen, mizu o kudasai) — with polite attention-getter

## Practice

1. **Observe**  
   Read: 水をください。 (mizu o kudasai)

2. **Break it**  
   水 … を … ください

3. **Pronounce**  
   Say the full phrase several times until smooth.

4. **Hide**  
   Produce it from the meaning “Water, please.”

5. **Compare with previous pattern**  
   - これください。 (this one, please)  
   - 水をください。 (water, please)

6. **Situation**  
   You are in a café or restaurant and want water. Ask for it. Then thank the person.

7. **Full short chain**  
   すみません、水をください。 → (receive water) → ありがとうございます。

## Proof of capability

Can you:

- Recognize the phrase?
- Say what it means?
- Produce 水をください。 without looking?
- Choose the more polite お水 version when appropriate?
- Add すみません in front when needed?
- Close with ありがとうございます after receiving it?

## Before

I could only ask for things by pointing and using これ/それ/あれ + ください. I had no simple way to name a concrete thing I wanted.

## After

I can ask for water with 水をください。 (mizu o kudasai) or the more polite お水をください。 (o-mizu o kudasai) and combine it with attention-getting and thanks.

## Status

new → growing (after practice)
