# S014 — One, Please

## Seed

basic-request (extension with quantity)

## Circumstance

You are in a shop, café, or restaurant and want one of something (or a specific small number). You want a simple way to say the quantity.

## Communicative objective

Be able to request a quantity using 一つ + ください.

## Core phrase

一つください。 (hitotsu kudasai)

## Meaning

One, please. / I’ll take one.

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- 一つ (hitotsu) — one (general counter for things)
- ください (kudasai) — please (give me)

### Structure

一つ + ください。

This is the same request frame as これください or 水をください, only a quantity is used instead of (or together with) a noun/demonstrative.

### Pronunciation

- 一つ (hi-to-tsu)

Smooth unit: **hitotsu kudasai**

### Context

Extremely useful in shops, cafés, convenience stores, and restaurants. Clear and polite. Works alone or combined with a noun (水を一つください).

## Variations

Keep them few and practical:

1. 一つください。 (hitotsu kudasai) — one, please (recommended core)
2. 二つください。 (futatsu kudasai) — two, please
3. 水を一つください。 (mizu o hitotsu kudasai) — one water, please (combining with a known noun)
4. すみません、一つください。 (sumimasen, hitotsu kudasai) — with polite attention-getter

## Practice

1. **Observe**  
   Read: 一つください。 (hitotsu kudasai)

2. **Break it**  
   一つ … ください

3. **Pronounce**  
   Say the full phrase several times until smooth.

4. **Hide**  
   Produce it from the meaning “One, please.”

5. **Compare / Combine**  
   - これください。 (this one, please)  
   - 一つください。 (one, please)  
   - 水を一つください。 (one water, please)

6. **Situation**  
   You want one of something (or one water). Ask for it. Then thank the person.

7. **Full short chain**  
   すみません、一つください。 → (receive it) → ありがとうございます。

## Proof of capability

Can you:

- Recognize the phrase?
- Say what it means?
- Produce 一つください。 without looking?
- Also produce 二つください。?
- Combine it with a known noun (水を一つください)?
- Close with ありがとうございます after receiving it?

## Before

I could request things and ask for water, but I had no simple way to specify a quantity.

## After

I can request a quantity with 一つください。 (hitotsu kudasai) or 二つください。 (futatsu kudasai), and I can combine it with known nouns like 水.

## Status

new → growing (after practice)

## Observations

- High reuse value: multiplies the power of every request already known.
- 一つ / 二つ are the most useful starting counters.
- Natural next step after concrete nouns in the request seed.
