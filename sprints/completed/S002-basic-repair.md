# S002 — Basic Repair

## Seed

basic-repair

## Circumstance

Someone is speaking to you in Japanese and you do not understand what they said. You need a simple, polite way to signal that and keep the interaction alive.

## Communicative objective

Be able to say that you do not understand, so the other person can adjust (repeat, slow down, use gestures, etc.).

## Core phrase

すみません、わかりません。 (sumimasen, wakarimasen)

## Meaning

Sorry, I don’t understand. / Excuse me, I don’t understand.

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- すみません (sumimasen) — excuse me / sorry (polite)
- わかりません (wakarimasen) — I don’t understand / I don’t know (polite negative of わかる)

### Structure

すみません、 + わかりません。

You can also say only わかりません。 (wakarimasen) if the situation is already clear.

### Grammar / pattern

- わかる (wakaru) = to understand / to know
- わかりません (wakarimasen) = polite negative present

Ready-made form is enough for now.

### Pronunciation

- すみません (su-mi-ma-sen)
- わかりません (wa-ka-ri-ma-sen)

Smooth unit: **sumimasen, wakarimasen**

### Context

Polite and safe with strangers, shop staff, etc. Buys time and keeps the interaction alive.

## Variations

1. すみません、わかりません。 (sumimasen, wakarimasen) — full polite version
2. わかりません。 (wakarimasen) — shorter
3. すみません。 (sumimasen) — just “excuse me”

## Practice summary

Observed, broken down, pronounced, recalled, varied (full/short), and used in a shop situation linked to S001.

## Proof of capability (completed)

User demonstrated:

- Correct recognition and meaning of both parts
- Accurate production of full and short versions without looking
- Natural use after a request (S001 + S002 chain)
- Realistic repair strategy: repeat the phrase + point again; acknowledge limits and external tools when needed

## Before

## After

I can politely say that I don’t understand using すみません、わかりません。 (sumimasen, wakarimasen) and give the other person a chance to help me. I can also chain it after a request.
