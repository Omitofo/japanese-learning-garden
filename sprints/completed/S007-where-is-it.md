# S007 — Where Is It?

## Seed

ask-where-something-is

## Circumstance

You need to find something and want a simple way to ask where it is.

## Communicative objective

Be able to ask where something is in a simple, polite way.

## Core phrase

これはどこですか。 (kore wa doko desu ka)

## Meaning

Where is this? / Where is it?

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- これ (kore) — this
- は (wa) — topic particle
- どこ (doko) — where
- です (desu) — polite copula
- か (ka) — question particle

### Pronunciation

**kore wa doko desu ka**

## Variations

1. これはどこですか。 (kore wa doko desu ka)
2. それはどこですか。 (sore wa doko desu ka)
3. あれはどこですか。 (are wa doko desu ka)

## Practice summary

Observed, broken down, pronounced, recalled, transformed (kore/sore/are), contrasted with 何 (nan), used in situation, and chained with repair tools.

## Proof of capability (completed)

User demonstrated:

- Correct recognition and meaning
- Clear distinction between 何 (nan) and どこ (doko)
- Accurate production of all three demonstrative variants
- Natural use with a map situation
- Full chain: ask what it is → ask where it is → repair toolkit when the answer is too fast

## Before

I had no simple way to ask where something was.

## After

I can ask where something is with これはどこですか。 (kore wa doko desu ka) and switch the demonstrative according to distance. I can also contrast it cleanly with the “what is it?” pattern and fall back to repair tools.
