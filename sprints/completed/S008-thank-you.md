# S008 — Thank You

## Seed

basic-politeness / thank-you

## Circumstance

You have just received help, an answer, an object, or any small service. You want to close the interaction politely.

## Communicative objective

Be able to thank someone in a simple, polite way.

## Core phrase

ありがとうございます。 (arigatou gozaimasu)

## Meaning

Thank you. / Thank you very much. (polite)

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- ありがとう (arigatou) — thank you
- ございます (gozaimasu) — polite ending

### Pronunciation

**arigatou gozaimasu** (long “tou”)

## Variations

1. ありがとうございます。 (arigatou gozaimasu) — standard polite
2. ありがとう。 (arigatou) — shorter, casual
3. すみません、ありがとうございます。 (sumimasen, arigatou gozaimasu)

## Practice summary

Observed, broken down, pronounced, recalled, varied, and used to close survival and information chains.

## Proof of capability (completed)

User demonstrated:

- Correct recognition and meaning
- Clear distinction between polite and casual forms
- Accurate production of all variants
- Natural closing after a request
- Full chain: request → ask what it is → don’t understand → speak slowly → thank you

## Before

I had no solid, automatic way to thank someone politely in Japanese.

## After

I can close an interaction politely with ありがとうございます。 (arigatou gozaimasu) and combine it with everything I already know.

## Status

growing
