# S005 — Speak More Slowly

## Seed

basic-repair (extension)

## Circumstance

Someone is speaking too fast for you to follow. You want them to slow down.

## Communicative objective

Be able to politely ask someone to speak more slowly.

## Core phrase

ゆっくり話してください。 (yukkuri hanashite kudasai)

## Meaning

Please speak slowly. / Please talk more slowly.

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- ゆっくり (yukkuri) — slowly
- 話して (hanashite) — speak (te-form)
- ください (kudasai) — please

### Pronunciation

- ゆっくり (yuk-ku-ri) — note the small っ
- 話して (ha-na-shi-te)

Smooth unit: **yukkuri hanashite kudasai**

## Variations

1. ゆっくり話してください。 (yukkuri hanashite kudasai)
2. ゆっくりお願いします。 (yukkuri onegaishimasu)
3. ゆっくり。 (yukkuri)
