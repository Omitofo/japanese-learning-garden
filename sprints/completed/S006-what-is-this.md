# S006 — What Is This?

## Seed

ask-what-something-is

## Circumstance

You see an object and do not know what it is. You want a simple way to ask while pointing.

## Communicative objective

Be able to ask what something is, especially while pointing.

## Core phrase

これは何ですか。 (kore wa nan desu ka)

## Meaning

What is this?

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- これ (kore) — this
- は (wa) — topic particle
- 何 (nan) — what
- です (desu) — polite copula
- か (ka) — question particle

### Pronunciation

**kore wa nan desu ka**

## Variations

1. これは何ですか。 (kore wa nan desu ka)
2. それは何ですか。 (sore wa nan desu ka)
3. あれは何ですか。 (are wa nan desu ka)

## Practice summary

Observed, broken down, pronounced, recalled, transformed (kore/sore/are), used in situation, and chained with repair tools.

## Proof of capability (completed)

User demonstrated:

- Correct recognition and meaning
- Accurate production of all three demonstrative variants
- Natural addition of すみません in a real situation
- Full realistic chain: ask what it is → don’t understand the answer → explain learning status → ask to speak slowly

## Before

I had no simple way to ask what something was.

## After

I can point at something and ask これは何ですか。 (kore wa nan desu ka). I can switch the demonstrative according to distance and fall back to my repair toolkit if needed.
