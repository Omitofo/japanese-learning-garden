## S009 — First concrete noun

- Replacing a demonstrative (これ/それ/あれ) with a concrete noun (トイレ) works cleanly inside the same question frame.
- The polite prefix お (o-) is available and natural for high-frequency service words like toilet.
- User spontaneously chose the more polite form + thanks in the test situation → good instinct.
